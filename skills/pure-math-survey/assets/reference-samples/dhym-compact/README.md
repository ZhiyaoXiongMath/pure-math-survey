# Compact dHYM adaptation

`dhym-surface-compact.tex` is a deliberately narrow, completed Part V-style example: the surface existence criterion and its Monge–Ampère reduction. Compile with the adjacent copy of `math-review.sty`. It preserves the general 12-point layout; it does not make the old 21-page article small by changing typography.

The main equivalence is stated in full in the introduction and recalled from one label-free macro before the proof. All branch and numerical conditions are retained. The proof develops the surface reduction and checks its converse branch. The scalar model is calculated, not merely named. The higher-dimensional numerical criteria, properness, individual scalar flows and categorical stability statements are outside this compact title and scope, not abridged until their hypotheses disappear.

The historical Thomas–Yau question is retained as context, with existence separated from smooth-flow convergence and surgery. It is not presented as a solved consequence of the surface criterion. No arbitrary new Problem is invented inside the already settled surface scope.

Provenance: a new editorial adaptation of the supplied 1.1.0 dHYM v6 sample, using its established surface references. The frozen original remains unchanged in `../dhym-v6/`. This is a layout/exposition maintenance sample, not a fresh comprehensive dHYM literature-status audit. The separately delivered Griffiths suite is the new-topic end-to-end test.
