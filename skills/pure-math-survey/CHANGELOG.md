## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-24

Developed from the user-supplied 1.0.1 archive. This is an implemented skill release, not another revision proposal.

### Content and reading contract

- Made actual principal-conclusion coverage mandatory in introductions, with selection, necessary hypotheses, local notation and bidirectional introduction/body review.
- Designated the supplied dHYM v6 as one completed-output benchmark for Part V. Added mandatory reference reading and separate content/presentation acceptance requirements for both V editions.
- Required developed body mechanisms and precise organizing-question/frontier treatment; preserved Part V's separate scope instead of importing Part III's full proof obligations.
- Implemented dependency order, first-use data/unknown/space/branch, complete explanations and no invented optional statement titles. Retained useful canonical repeats and original-number recalls.
- Updated the invocation prompt and every relevant reference document, including examples and existing-record integration.

### Templates and evidence

- Updated all ten part/edition templates. Part II and V place principal-statement slots inside the actual introduction; V now has a developed independent-article scaffold.
- Replaced the common style with the v6 profile, while keeping generic theorem environments and excluding subject-specific macros.
- Included frozen original v6 TeX/PDF with checksums and provenance. Historical mathematics and bibliography were not updated by this release.
- Expanded the elementary fixture to exact minimization and gradient-iteration conclusions, with one shared body for each and original-number recalls. Added adverse semantic-review cases.

### Tools and tests

- Added a no-shell-escape, reference-stabilizing 13-entry build runner; a whole-PDF render comparator; and a deterministic, checksummed ZIP packager rejecting unsafe paths, symlinks and font files.
- Preserved 52 existing regression tests and added 32 tests. The tests check contracts, references, fixtures, canonical-body structure and helper behavior, not mathematical comprehension.
- Kept the project schema at 1.0.0 and retained the structural validator, scope protocol, statuses and existing audit/registry system.

Actual test, build, layout and packaging evidence is recorded in `docs/release-verification.md`. No new-topic generation evaluation, independent mathematical referee review or fresh literature audit of the frozen reference is claimed.
