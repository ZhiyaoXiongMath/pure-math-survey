# Part V completed-output benchmark

## Status and required use

The user designated [dHYM v6](../assets/reference-samples/dhym-v6/README.md) as one reference for Part V, including formatting and typesetting. Both V editions must meet its quality level within their selected scope. It is a completed article reference, not merely a skeleton, not the union of I–IV obligations, and not a claim that every future output automatically matches it.

Before drafting V, read this file and the reference's introduction, then inspect a body passage matching the intended kind of explanation and its PDF pages. For a mathematically unrelated topic, learn the exposition and layout patterns without importing its symbols, results, bibliography, section names or problem choices. After drafting, compare the whole article's actual reading experience and rendered pages with this benchmark. Record a brief account in the existing architecture and release evidence. Read the full sample on demand, not as mandatory context for every I–IV-only request.

## What has to transfer

| Dimension | Required outcome | Failure that must be repaired |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction knowledge | Actual selected main conclusions, local definitions, hypotheses, quantifiers, branch/range and source status | A roadmap, author list, result names, or one full theorem with other main threads missing |
| Independent article | One coherent question, notation system, abstract, introduction, numbering, contents and bibliography | References to absent volumes carrying essential content; concatenated summaries |
| Developed body | Results organized by mathematical tasks, real comparisons, useful examples and the relevant mechanisms | A theorem catalog below a good introduction; length without explanatory development |
| Mechanism explanation | The input, transformation/estimate/construction, actual output and its use by the next step | Method labels or unexplained arrows; a peripheral proof hiding a missing central bridge |
| Boundary and frontier | Organizing question, decisive progress and restrictions; local Problems in their proper role | Local questions replacing the central question; historical conjecture treated as automatically still open |
| Statement precision | Essential assumptions and logical force retained; sources/versions qualified | Dropped initial-data or branch conditions; arbitrary stability notions conflated |
| Repetition | One label-free canonical body for repeated full statements; original-number recalls | Two drifting copies or new numbering for the same recalled theorem |
| Presentation | The shared v6 profile, readable formulas/lists, stable links and deliberate page breaks | Global font/margin compression, clipping, missing glyphs, stranded headings or formula labels |

Short derivations that reveal a decisive connection belong in V when necessary for the selected understanding. A proof sketch may import an accurately stated external result, but must identify the actual role and check the hypotheses. This does not impose a complete proof of every source theorem or activate Part III's entire route inventory.

A genuine one-result scope can be shorter; a broad multi-thread survey must not omit principal knowledge to imitate a short article. Do not add the Thomas--Yau problem to unrelated topics: identify the organizing questions that actually belong to those topics.

## Introductory knowledge and body development are both required

A reader of the introduction alone should be able to answer: what is the question, what are the selected answers, under what conditions, with what logical strength, and what prevents stronger conclusions? The body should then explain the real relationships and mechanisms, not merely repeat the statements.

Use a compact full theorem when practical. A precise explicitly limited special case is allowed when it carries the selected main knowledge without obscuring a material wider range. Important counterexamples, convergence distinctions and scope limitations can appear as formulas or short sourced statements rather than being forced into a theorem environment. The intro/body mapping belongs in the existing architecture note.

## Presentation and regression

[Layout and build](layout-and-build.md) specifies the shared profile extracted from v6. A regression build replaces only the sample's preamble with `math-review.sty`, keeps its mathematical source unchanged, and compares the result with an original-preamble rebuild and the supplied PDF. The immutable originals are never overwritten.

This tests style compatibility on a developed article. It does not show that the skill has generated a new research survey, verified the literature again, passed independent mathematical review or generalized successfully to every field. Report those as separate checks and do not substitute a pixel match for reader-outcomes review.

## Acceptance in existing records

Use `reader_outcomes` for introduction-only reading, reverse coverage, independent article structure and actual method interfaces. Use `edition_depth` for focused but complete concise treatment and deeper standard explanation. Use `statement_verification` and semantic consistency for exact claims and repeated bodies; `frontier_status` for organizing questions and current-state qualifications. Build and visual evidence must identify the real PDFs and all inspected pages.

A missing core conclusion, unfulfilled central mechanism, incorrect restriction or unresolved substantial layout defect is a repair item, not a limitation that can be waived by prose. A separately disclosed limit of independent review or broad empirical testing does not certify or invalidate an otherwise completed maintenance check.
## Compact default

For new concise work, use the compact dHYM adaptation as the length/selection demonstration and the frozen v6 as an optional depth reference. The 21-page original is not the expected length of a new manuscript. Read the full sample progressively: introduction, one relevant mechanism, one boundary; do not import all its branches or load it as current evidence.
